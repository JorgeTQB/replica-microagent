### Design Decisions
1. **No unassigned classes** — all 0 remaining classes were accounted for.
2. **No anti-patterns** introduced — each service has a clear single responsibility aligned to a business domain.
3. **Common classes** (infrastructure/cross-cutting) are replicated across microservices as each would be independently deployed.
4. **`OrderStatus`** is shared between Order Management and Shipment & Delivery since shipment state changes depend on order state progression (Shipped, Delivered, Installed statuses).
5. **Exception classes** are only included in services that actually use them, avoiding unnecessary coupling.
