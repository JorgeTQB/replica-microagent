## Analysis Summary

Based on thorough investigation of the codebase, I can identify the following core business domains from this ordering/supply chain application:

1. **Product Catalog Management** - Managing catalog items (products/parts) with SKU numbers, descriptions, prices, inventory, and lead times. This includes the full lifecycle of catalog item management.

2. **Dealer Management** - Managing dealer/supplier information including contact details, addresses, and email/phone. Dealers are associated with quotes and form a critical part of the business network.

3. **Quote Management** - Creating and managing customer price quotes that include catalog items, pricing, discounts, validity periods, and delivery locations. Quotes are associated with dealers and serve as the basis for orders.

4. **Order Management** - Processing orders created from approved quotes, tracking order status, maintaining event history (audit trail), and managing the order lifecycle from creation to fulfillment.

5. **Shipment Management** - Managing physical delivery of ordered goods including shipment records, delivery addresses, contact information, delivery dates, and shipment event tracking.

The application is a "Parts Unlimited" ordering service with a clear supply chain flow:
- Products exist in the **Catalog** → 
- **Dealers** associate with **Quotes** → 
- **Quotes** convert to **Orders** → 
- **Orders** fulfill via **Shipments**

Each domain has:
- Its own controller (REST API layer)
- Its own repository interface and implementations (data layer)
- Its own domain models
- Its own database tables
