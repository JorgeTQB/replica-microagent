### Design Decisions:
- **No unassigned classes**: All 20 classes in the application are properly assigned to domains.
- **AbstractActionBean** is shared across all 3 microservices as it's the foundation base class for all web controllers.
- **CatalogService** is shared in Account Management (fetching favorite products) and Shopping Cart & Order Management (item lookups + stock checks), while being the primary service in Catalog.
- **Item + ItemMapper** are shared in Catalog (primary entities) and Shopping Cart & Order Management (inventory management during order processing).
- Each microservice has clear, well-defined boundaries following Domain-Driven Design principles with no anti-patterns detected.
