### Key Design Decisions
- **`AbstractActionBean`** replicated across all 3 services — it's a base presentation class required by all action beans
- **`CatalogService`, `ItemMapper`, `Item`** shared across all 3 services — catalog lookups are needed for account personalization, cart item validation, and order item details
- **`Account`** shared with Shopping Cart & Order — `Order.initOrder()` requires account billing/shipping info to populate the order
- **No unassigned classes** — all 22 unique classes from the monolith are properly placed in domain-driven microservice boundaries
