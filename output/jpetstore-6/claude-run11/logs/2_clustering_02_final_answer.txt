Based on my thorough analysis of the codebase, I can now identify all classes that belong to the Catalog domain. Let me compile the final list:

**Domain Objects (Data Model):**

- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Product` - Represents products within categories
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Item` - Represents individual inventory items with pricing and supplier info

**Business Layer:**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.service.CatalogService` - Business logic for catalog operations (browsing, search, inventory checks)

**Presentation Layer:**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.web.actions.CatalogActionBean` - Web controller handling catalog browsing and search

**Data Access Layer (Mappers):**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.mapper.CategoryMapper` - Data access for categories
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.mapper.ProductMapper` - Data access for products (including search)
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.mapper.ItemMapper` - Data access for items and inventory

**Note on ItemMapper:** While it's also used by OrderService for updating inventory quantities upon order placement, it's primarily a Catalog domain data access class (managing item data and inventory).
