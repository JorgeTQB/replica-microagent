Based on my thorough analysis of the application, I now have a comprehensive understanding of the JPetStore monolithic application. Let me synthesize my findings:

**Application Overview:**
- This is the classic JPetStore application - an online pet store
- Database has 13 tables covering: user accounts, product catalog, inventory, shopping cart (session-based), orders, and suppliers
- The application is organized into domain objects, mappers (data access), services (business logic), and web actions (presentation)

**Key Findings:**

1. **Account/User Management Domain**: Account, AccountService, AccountMapper, AccountActionBean - handles user registration, login/logout, profile management, authentication

2. **Catalog Domain**: Category, Product, Item (inventory), CatalogService, CategoryMapper, ProductMapper, ItemMapper, CatalogActionBean - handles browsing categories, products, items, and searching

3. **Shopping Cart Domain**: Cart, CartItem, CartActionBean - manages the shopping cart session, adding/removing items, quantity management

4. **Order Domain**: Order, LineItem, OrderService, OrderMapper, LineItemMapper, SequenceMapper, OrderActionBean - handles order creation, order history, payment processing

The Cart domain has a close relationship with the Order domain (Order uses Cart to initialize), but they represent different business capabilities (cart management vs. order processing/fulfillment). The Catalog domain underpins both Cart and Order but is a distinct capability for browsing and searching products.
