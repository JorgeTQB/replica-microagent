## Analysis Summary

**Current 4 Domains:**
1. **Account Management**: Account, AccountService, AccountMapper, AccountActionBean, AbstractActionBean
2. **Catalog**: Category, Product, Item, CatalogService, CatalogActionBean, CategoryMapper, ProductMapper, ItemMapper
3. **Shopping Cart**: Cart, CartItem, CartActionBean
4. **Order Management**: Order, LineItem, Sequence, OrderService, OrderActionBean, OrderMapper, LineItemMapper, SequenceMapper

**Key Dependencies Observed:**

1. **Shopping Cart → Catalog**: `CartActionBean` uses `CatalogService` to fetch items and check stock. `Cart` uses `Item`. This is a strong dependency on Catalog domain.

2. **Order Management → Shopping Cart**: `OrderActionBean` directly calls `CartActionBean` to get the cart for order creation, and clears the cart after order. `Order.initOrder()` takes both `Account` and `Cart` as parameters.

3. **Order Management → Account**: `OrderActionBean` uses `AccountActionBean` to get user data.

4. **Order Management → Catalog**: `OrderService` uses `ItemMapper` to update inventory quantities.

5. **Account Management → Catalog**: `AccountActionBean` uses `CatalogService` to fetch favorite category products.

**Merging Strategy to Get Exactly 3 Clusters:**

The natural groupings are:
- **Account Management** stands relatively independently - handles user identity and preferences
- **Catalog** is the core read-heavy domain  
- **Shopping Cart** and **Order Management** are strongly coupled - `OrderActionBean` calls `CartActionBean`, `Order.initOrder()` takes `Cart`, and `OrderService` manages inventory via `ItemMapper`

The best merge is **Shopping Cart + Order Management** → `Order & Cart Management` because:
- `OrderActionBean` directly accesses `CartActionBean` from the session
- `Order.initOrder(Account, Cart)` tightly couples them
- After order placement, the cart is cleared in `OrderActionBean.newOrder()`
- Both domains together represent the transaction/purchase flow

This results in exactly **3 clusters**:
1. **Account Management** (unchanged)
2. **Catalog** (unchanged)  
3. **Order & Cart Management** (Shopping Cart + Order Management merged)
