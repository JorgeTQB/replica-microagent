### Design Rationale for Shared Classes:
- **`AbstractActionBean`** → Shared across all 3 microservices as it is the base class for all action beans
- **`CatalogService`** + **`Item`** + **`ItemMapper`** + **`Product`** → Included in Account Management (for personalized `myList` via `getProductListByCategory()`) and in Order & Cart Management (for `CartActionBean` to look up items and check stock via `CatalogService`)
- **`Account`** → Included in Order & Cart Management because `Order.initOrder(Account, Cart)` directly uses Account data to populate shipping/billing info
