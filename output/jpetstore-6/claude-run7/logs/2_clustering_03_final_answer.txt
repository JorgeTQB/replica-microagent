**Core Shopping Cart domain classes:**
1. `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Cart` - The main cart domain object
2. `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.CartItem` - Items within the cart
3. `org.mybatis.jpetstore.web.actions.CartActionBean` - Presentation layer (web action handler)

**Classes that are related but belong to OTHER domains:**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Item` - Belongs to Catalog domain
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.LineItem` - Belongs to Order Management domain
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Order` - Belongs to Order Management domain
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.service.CatalogService` - Belongs to Catalog domain (used by CartActionBean to fetch item data)
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.web.actions.OrderActionBean` - Belongs to Order Management domain
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.web.actions.AbstractActionBean` - Shared base class used by all action beans (not specific to Shopping Cart)

The Shopping Cart domain has no dedicated service class (it uses `CatalogService` for item lookups) and no dedicated mapper/repository (since the cart is session-scoped/transient and not persisted to the database directly).

The Shopping Cart domain consists exclusively of:
- **Domain Objects**: `Cart`, `CartItem`
- **Presentation Layer**: `CartActionBean`
