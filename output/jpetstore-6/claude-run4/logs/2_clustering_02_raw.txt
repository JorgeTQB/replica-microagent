Based on my thorough analysis, I have identified all classes belonging to the Catalog domain. Let me summarize my findings:

**Domain Objects:**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Category` - Category entity (used exclusively by CatalogService)
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Product` - Product entity (used by CatalogService and CatalogActionBean)
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain.Item` - Item entity (core catalog item with pricing, supplier, attributes)

**Business Layer:**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.service.CatalogService` - Core catalog business logic (catalog browsing, search, inventory check)

**Presentation Layer:**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.web.actions.CatalogActionBean` - Web action bean for catalog browsing

**Data Layer (Mappers):**
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.mapper.CategoryMapper` - Data access for categories
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.mapper.ProductMapper` - Data access for products (including search)
- `org.mybatis.jpetstore.mapper.ItemMapper` - Data access for items and inventory

Note: `ItemMapper` is also used by `OrderService`, but its primary domain is Catalog as it handles catalog item retrieval and inventory management. The `Item` domain object is shared but belongs primarily to the Catalog domain. `AbstractActionBean` is shared infrastructure and not catalog-specific.
