### 🔍 Key Design Decisions

1. **No unassigned classes** — All 20 classes in the application were properly assigned.
2. **AbstractActionBean** appears in all 3 microservices as it is the infrastructure base class extended by all 4 ActionBeans across all domains.
3. **CatalogService, Item, ItemMapper** appear in all 3 microservices because:
   - Catalog: Primary owner (core catalog browsing functionality)
   - Account Management: Used to fetch the favorites/product list (`getProductListByCategory`)
   - Shopping Cart & Order Mgmt: Used for stock checking (`isItemInStock`) and item lookup (`getItem`)
4. **Product** appears in Account Management and Catalog — used for the user's `myList` favorites feature.
5. **Account** appears in Account Management and Shopping Cart & Order Mgmt — `Order.initOrder(account, cart)` requires account data to populate billing/shipping info.
6. **Anti-patterns avoided**: Each microservice is self-contained with its own domain data, services, mappers, and presentation layer. No "God Service", no anemic services, clear bounded contexts maintained.
