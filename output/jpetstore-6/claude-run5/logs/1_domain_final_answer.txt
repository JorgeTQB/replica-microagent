## Analysis Summary

Based on my thorough investigation of the JPetStore application, I can identify the following core business domains:

### 1. **Account Management Domain**
- Handles user registration, authentication, profile management, and preferences
- Database tables: `account`, `signon`, `profile`, `bannerdata`
- Core classes: Account domain, AccountService, AccountMapper, AccountActionBean
- Clear bounded context: user identity, authentication, and profile preferences

### 2. **Catalog Domain**
- Manages the product catalog including categories, products, and items/inventory
- Database tables: `category`, `product`, `item`, `inventory`, `supplier`
- Core classes: Category, Product, Item domain objects, CatalogService, CategoryMapper, ItemMapper, ProductMapper, CatalogActionBean
- Clear bounded context: browsing and searching the product catalog with stock checking

### 3. **Shopping Cart Domain**
- Manages the shopping cart lifecycle - adding/removing/updating items before checkout
- Core classes: Cart, CartItem domain objects, CartActionBean
- Clear bounded context: temporary shopping session management before order placement

### 4. **Order Management Domain**
- Handles order creation, management, and fulfillment tracking
- Database tables: `orders`, `orderstatus`, `lineitem`, `sequence`
- Core classes: Order, LineItem, Sequence domain objects, OrderService, OrderMapper, LineItemMapper, SequenceMapper, OrderActionBean
- Clear bounded context: order lifecycle from placement to fulfillment tracking

These four domains have distinct responsibilities, different data ownership, and clear boundaries. The Shopping Cart and Order domains could potentially be merged, but they serve different purposes (transient session state vs. persisted order records) and have different lifecycles.
