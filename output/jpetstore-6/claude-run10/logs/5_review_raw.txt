### Key Design Decisions

1. **`AbstractActionBean`** assigned to all 3 microservices — It is the infrastructure base class that all web controllers (ActionBeans) extend. Each microservice owns its own ActionBeans, so they all need this base class.

2. **`CatalogService`** assigned to Account Management + Shopping Cart + Catalog Management — It is the primary owner in Catalog Management, but is also a **dependency** for the other two. In a true microservices architecture, Account Management and Shopping Cart microservices would call the Catalog microservice via REST API for their needed catalog operations.

3. **No unassigned classes** — All 19 unique classes in the system have been appropriately assigned to their respective domain clusters.

4. **Clear bounded contexts** with distinct database tables:
   - Account Management → `account`, `signon`, `profile`, `bannerdata`
   - Catalog Management → `category`, `product`, `item`, `inventory`, `supplier`
   - Shopping Cart & Order Management → `orders`, `orderstatus`, `lineitem`, `sequence`
