Based on my thorough analysis of the codebase, I can now identify all the domain-specific classes for the Catalog Management domain. Let me compile the final list:

**Presentation Layer:**
- `CatalogActionBean` - handles catalog browsing, category/product/item viewing, and search

**Business Layer:**
- `CatalogService` - business logic for catalog operations (search, browse by category, item availability)

**Data Layer (Mappers):**
- `CategoryMapper` - data access for categories
- `ProductMapper` - data access for products
- `ItemMapper` - data access for items and inventory

**Domain Objects:**
- `Category` - represents a product category (e.g., Fish, Dogs)
- `Product` - represents a product within a category
- `Item` - represents a specific SKU with pricing, attributes, supplier info, and inventory

Note: `AbstractActionBean` is a shared base class used across multiple domains (Account, Cart, Order, Catalog), so it belongs to common/support classes, not exclusively to Catalog Management. The `ItemMapper` is used by both CatalogService and OrderService, but since it primarily serves catalog data (item lookup, inventory query), and inventory deduction by OrderService is a cross-domain operation, `ItemMapper` is core to Catalog Management.
