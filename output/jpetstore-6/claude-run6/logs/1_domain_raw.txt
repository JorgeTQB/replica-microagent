## Analysis Summary

**Application**: JPetStore - an online pet store application

**Key Packages**:
1. `org.mybatis.jpetstore.domain` - Domain model classes
2. `org.mybatis.jpetstore.mapper` - Data access layer (MyBatis mappers)
3. `org.mybatis.jpetstore.service` - Business logic services
4. `org.mybatis.jpetstore.web.actions` - Web presentation layer

**Core Business Capabilities Identified**:

1. **Account/User Management**: Handles user registration, authentication (sign-on/sign-off), profile management, and preferences. The `AccountService`, `AccountMapper`, `Account` domain class, and `AccountActionBean` form a coherent bounded context around user identity and profile management. Database tables: `account`, `signon`, `profile`, `bannerdata`.

2. **Product Catalog**: Manages browsing and searching of categories, products, and items. The `CatalogService`, `CategoryMapper`, `ProductMapper`, `ItemMapper`, and related domain objects (`Category`, `Product`, `Item`) form a natural catalog bounded context. Database tables: `category`, `product`, `item`, `supplier`, `inventory`.

3. **Shopping Cart**: Manages the shopping cart experience - adding/removing/updating items before checkout. The `Cart`, `CartItem` domain objects and `CartActionBean` form a distinct cart management context that serves as an intermediate step between browsing and ordering.

4. **Order Management**: Handles the full order lifecycle - creating orders from carts, persisting orders, tracking order status and line items. The `OrderService`, `OrderMapper`, `LineItemMapper`, `Order`, `LineItem` domain objects, and `OrderActionBean` form a bounded context around order processing. Database tables: `orders`, `orderstatus`, `lineitem`, `sequence`.
