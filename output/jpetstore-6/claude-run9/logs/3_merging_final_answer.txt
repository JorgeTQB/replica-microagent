4. **Order Management** - `OrderService` uses `ItemMapper` (from Catalog domain) to update inventory. `Order.initOrder()` takes `Account` and `Cart` as parameters.

**Key Cross-Domain Dependencies:**
- `CartActionBean` → `CatalogService` (Cart depends on Catalog for item info/stock)
- `OrderActionBean` → `CartActionBean` + `AccountActionBean` (Order depends on Cart and Account)
- `OrderService` → `ItemMapper` (Order depends on Catalog for inventory updates)
- `Order.initOrder()` → `Account` + `Cart` (Order depends on both Account and Cart)

**Merging Strategy (to form exactly 3 clusters):**

1. **Account Management** remains as its own microservice (clear identity/user bounded context)
2. **Catalog** remains as its own microservice (clear product/inventory bounded context)  
3. **Shopping Cart + Order Management** → Merge these two, as they are tightly coupled:
   - Cart is the pre-purchase state that feeds into Order creation
   - `OrderActionBean` directly calls `CartActionBean` and uses Cart data
   - `Order.initOrder()` takes Cart as parameter
   - The checkout flow naturally bridges both domains
   - This forms a "Commerce/Transaction" microservice
