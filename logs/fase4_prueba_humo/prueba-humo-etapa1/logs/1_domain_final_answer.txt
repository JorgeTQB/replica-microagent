**Application Overview**: JPetStore is an online pet store application built with MyBatis and Stripes framework. It manages user accounts, a product catalog, shopping cart, and order processing.

**Key Business Domains Identified**:

1. **Account Management**: Handles user registration, authentication (sign-on/sign-off), profile management, and user preferences (language, favorite category, banner options). This domain is served by the `AccountService`, `AccountMapper`, `Account` domain class, and `AccountActionBean`. The database tables involved are `account`, `signon`, `profile`, and `bannerdata`.

2. **Catalog Management**: Manages the product catalog including categories, products, items, and inventory. This domain handles browsing, searching, and viewing items from the catalog hierarchy (Category → Product → Item). It's served by `CatalogService`, `CategoryMapper`, `ProductMapper`, `ItemMapper`, domain classes `Category`, `Product`, `Item`, and `CatalogActionBean`. The database tables involved are `category`, `product`, `item`, `inventory`, and `supplier`.

3. **Shopping Cart**: Manages the customer's shopping session - adding/removing items, updating quantities, and tracking what the user intends to buy before checkout. This domain is centered around `Cart`, `CartItem`, and `CartActionBean`.

4. **Order Management**: Handles the full order lifecycle - creating orders from a cart, managing order status, tracking line items, and viewing order history. This is served by `OrderService`, `OrderMapper`, `LineItemMapper`, `SequenceMapper`, domain classes `Order`, `LineItem`, `Sequence`, and `OrderActionBean`. The database tables involved are `orders`, `orderstatus`, `lineitem`, and `sequence`.
